Date: 2026-11-07. Direct conversation evidence, retained explicitly.

rendering and models for transformed lists, configured Metro for HTTP, jaqt for
object-array queries and in model effects when it simplifies the code. They
supply poef/solid-apps as the reference, then request inclusion of the moldable

shared features. Do not require a separate pilot approval or re-prove established
architecture. Normal regression/integration checks and preservation of data and
library contracts remain required. The IDE itself and unrelated roadmap features
remain outside the proposed refactor. No cycle acceptance or merge is stated.
