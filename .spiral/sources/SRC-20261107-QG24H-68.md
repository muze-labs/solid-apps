# Next-cycle selection: session renewal and recovery

The maintainer requests next-cycle candidates after accepting shared UI CYC49.
The assessment recommends session renewal and recovery, with Preferences UI
among the alternatives. The maintainer responds "Agreed" and then explicitly
answers the clarification "Session renewal and recovery".

Interpretation: select the session-recovery cycle rather than Preferences UI.
The prior roadmap calls for refresh-first behavior, understandable sign-in
recovery and preserved edits/saves. The selection does not itself confirm a
particular new authentication mechanism or authorize publication/integration.

Source context: earlier live Firefox diagnostics established shared expired
authorization without a refresh token; reconnecting resolved the symptom.
The maintainer had asked whether silent renewal could precede an explicit
sign-in request and directed adding that work to the roadmap.

After the concrete understanding and evidenced gap were presented, the maintainer
answers "Yes, use refresh-first recovery". The confirmed proposal requests
refresh authorization where supported, lets Metro renew silently, then offers
Sign in again; it retains drafts and conditional saves, distinguishes other
failures and avoids replaying completed writes. Hidden-iframe sign-in remains
later work. This confirms implementation, not acceptance or integration.


## Maintainer's live recovery evaluation and toast feedback

The maintainer confirms seeing Sign in again, successful sign-in and its toast,
with a reasonable timeout: "looks good". They identify the remaining abruptness:
"the toast doesn't have an animation" and request popping up, then going back
down after a while. This is evidence of the live recovery UI, not evidence that
a refresh token was issued or that later silent renewal occurred.

Interpretation: local polish to the shared recovery confirmation in this Active
cycle: brief slide/fade entry and exit, preserving the accepted notification
lifetime and existing accessibility/focus behavior. No commit/merge instruction
or blanket cycle acceptance is inferred from this feedback.
