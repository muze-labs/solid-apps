## Research and resulting choices

Primary sources inspected 2026-11-07:

- WAI button pattern: https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/button/
  Dialog opening is a button action. Ellipsis is a convention; no icon is
  required. Use a native button with descriptive accessible name, Space/Enter,
  aria-haspopup=dialog, and hover/focus affordance. Keep the visible name plain.
- WAI dialog pattern:
  https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/dialog-modal/
  Move focus inside on opening and return it to the invoker on closing.
- WCAG status messages:
  https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/status-messages.html
  Expose non-interrupting status updates to assistive technology without moving
  focus. The shared toast keeps a polite atomic status region in the document.
- WCAG timing:
  https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/timing-adjustable.html
  Expiring feedback must not be the only access to essential information or
  time-limited actions. Timed toasts are restricted to nonessential completion
  confirmations whose results remain visible in app state. Recovery errors,
  sign-in instructions and conflict guidance stay inline. No timed action exists.
- Material snackbar guidance:
  https://m3.material.io/components/snackbar/overview
  Brief process updates should avoid interrupting work; they can be temporary or
  persistent. Installed the-ds 0.2.7 supplies toast styling, but not the required
  accessible SimplyFlow rendering/lifecycle. A small dataset fills that gap.

No unverified screen-reader compliance claim follows from these sources or DOM
checks. Laws of UX llms.txt was attempted but inaccessible via web retrieval on
this update; the earlier cycle's reading/guidance remains applicable.

## Implementation and checks

Shared toast is authored HTML/styles and SimplyFlow data/actions/behavior. Native
focus and timers are confined to lifecycle, with behavior cleanup. Confirmation
call sites opt into notify explicitly; errors remain message/inline. Preferences
has its own instance inside the modal, preserving the modal accessibility tree.
Toasts do not animate or automatically take focus. Hover/focus pauses expiration;
manual keyboard dismissal restores previous focus. Replacing a notice cancels
its old timer. The current design shows the most recent completion notice.
